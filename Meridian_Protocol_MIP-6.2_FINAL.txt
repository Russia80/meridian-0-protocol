## Motivation and Scope

This document formalises the integration of **Claude AI** as an external oracle within our $L9$ consensus framework via **Predictive Arbitration**. It further introduces **Alice AI** as a dedicated cognitive fact‑checking layer for STEM‑use cases, explicitly designed to enforce **human‑aligned validation** before any system fallback occurs.

The primary objective is to reduce fallback thresholds from ±25% to <±10%, ensuring that economic finality ($E > 0$) is preserved even under extreme stress scenarios (S4). A secondary objective is to institutionalise a clear responsibility boundary between predictive oracles and the human‑facing validation layer, aligning with principles of distributed accountability in AI‑assisted decision‑making.

## Algorithm Flow (Predictive Arbitration Logic)

1. **Deviation Calculation:** Compute current deviation and update dynamic weights based on MSE across oracles.
2. **Level 1 Warning:** If $Δ_{avg} > 10\%$, emit alert and log detailed metrics for review.
3. **Level 2 Prediction:** If predicted drift $>15\%$ (based on sliding‑window forecast), trigger Predictive Arbitration and prepare fallback candidates.
4. **Level 3 Fact Check:** Before any fallback execution, **Alice AI** performs mandatory cognitive fact‑check on core data points.
   - If Alice disputes critical inputs, **fallback is blocked immediately** and a DAO vote is required to override.
   - This mechanism enforces a clear **human‑in‑the‑loop checkpoint** and codifies distributed responsibility between predictive models and the validation layer.

## Security & Governance

Thresholds are set by the **Infrastructure Lead** and ratified in DAO voting. **Alice AI's fact-checking veto power** triggers mandatory logging and can escalate to DAO review if disputed facts persist.
